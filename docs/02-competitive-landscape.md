## 2.2 How the market is moving (and what it means for CHRM)

1. **Consolidation toward Salesforce.** Momentum (Mar 2026) is the tenth Agentforce-oriented acquisition in six months. Salesloft + Clari merged (Dec 2025). The HubSpot/Pipedrive mid-market is being left to notetakers and native features. CHRM should say so explicitly and run a "Momentum alternative for HubSpot" page while the search demand exists.
2. **Native CRM AI suggests, it doesn't execute.** HubSpot's Smart Deal Progression requires rep approval on every update and only ships on Pro+. Pipedrive shipped an MCP server instead of a notetaker. Both make "execution layer" a real, defensible layer rather than a feature the CRM will absorb next quarter.
3. **AI-native CRMs are winning the narrative but losing on migration cost.** Attio raised prices in July 2026 and meters AI via credits; every capability beyond record-keeping needs workflows or agents you configure. Their audience (founders who want AI in the CRM) overlaps CHRM's ICP exactly. The message is "keep your CRM, get the AI."
4. **Notetakers are moving up.** tl;dv's 2026 "AI Agents" drafts follow-ups and updates deals. Fathom holds 20k+ HubSpot installs. CHRM should keep them as ingestion partners and own the work above them: sending, LinkedIn, enrichment, committee, risk, iteration.
5. **Outcome-based pricing is arriving.** HubSpot charges $0.50/resolved conversation and $1/qualified lead; Agentforce $2/conversation. CHRM's flat $1,000/AE/month is simpler and should be positioned as "a second AE for one-twelfth of the cost of hiring one," not as a per-seat SaaS fee.

## 2.3 Positioning map (two axes the buyer actually uses)

```
                 Executes the after-call work end to end
                                 ▲
        Rox (SF, enterprise)     │      CHRM (HubSpot / Pipedrive, live in a day)
        Attention (enterprise)   │      Oliv.ai (modular agents)
        Momentum → Salesforce    │
   ──────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────►
   Requires migration /          │                Works on the CRM you have
   engineering / implementation  │
        Attio, Day.ai, Clarify   │      Sybill, Airspeed, Winn.ai (fields + drafts)
        DIY GTM stack            │      Fathom, Fireflies, tl;dv (notes)
                                 │      HubSpot Breeze, Pipedrive AI (suggests)
                                 ▼
                 Records, summarises or suggests; a human still executes
```

CHRM's claim is the top-right quadrant alone. Nobody else combines "no migration, no engineer, live in a day" with "does the whole job."

## 2.4 Messaging lines per alternative (for compare pages and sales)

- **AI-native CRMs:** "Everything Attio promises, on the HubSpot or Pipedrive you already run. No migration. No workflow canvas. No engineer."
- **Rox / Attention / Agentforce:** "Enterprise agent platforms want a data cloud, an implementation partner and a quarter. CHRM wants four OAuth clicks and a 30-minute call."
- **Momentum:** "Momentum is Salesforce now. If you're on HubSpot or Pipedrive, CHRM is where the execution layer lives."
- **Oliv / Sybill / Airspeed / Winn:** "They fill fields and draft follow-ups. CHRM sends the follow-up, works the committee on LinkedIn, enriches the contacts, flags the risk and learns from the outcome. A second AE, not a smarter notetaker."
- **Fathom / Fireflies / tl;dv:** "Keep your notetaker. CHRM takes its transcript and does the rest of the job."
- **HubSpot Breeze / Pipedrive AI:** "Your CRM's AI suggests. CHRM executes. And it works on Starter, and on Pipedrive."
- **DIY GTM stack:** "A GTM engineer costs more than every AE seat on CHRM combined, and the wiring breaks every time an API changes."

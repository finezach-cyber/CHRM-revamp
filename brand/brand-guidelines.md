# CHRM brand guidelines (2026-09 refresh)

## The idea
CHRM is **CRM with the H put back in**: the human. The product does the execution so the human can do the two things software never will: build trust and provide direction. The logo says this literally. The C, R and M are ink. The H is the only thing in colour.

- Promise: **Every closer gets a second AE.**
- Category: The execution layer for CRM.
- Brand line (sign-off, About, footer): Stay human. Let AI do the rest.
- Always beside the promise: works with the CRM you already have. HubSpot or Pipedrive. CHRM is not a CRM.

## Logo
- `logo-wordmark.svg`: geometric wordmark, 16px stroke. H in CHRM blue. Use on paper/white.
- `logo-wordmark-white.svg`: for ink backgrounds. H in the light blue tint so it still reads.
- `logo-mark.svg`: rounded square, ink ground, C in paper, H in blue. App icon, social avatar.
- `favicon.svg`: the H alone on ink. 16–64px.
- Clear space: the height of the H on all sides. Never recolour the H to ink; never colour the other letters.

## Colour
| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| paper | #FAFAF7 | page ground |
| ink | #0A0A0A | text, primary buttons |
| accent (CHRM blue) | #2B4BE8 | the H, links on hover, one highlighted column, eyebrow labels for CHRM's side. Matches the product UI's blue so marketing and app feel like one company. AA on white (6.4:1). |
| accent-soft | #E8ECFF | the CHRM column in comparison tables, verdict blocks |
| done | #1E7B4E | "written", "sent" chips only |
| risk | #8A5A00 | "flagged" chips only |

Rule: one accent per screen. If two things are blue, one of them is wrong. Green and amber are never used for decoration, only to mean a thing was done or a deal is at risk.

## Type
- Display: Instrument Serif, 400, tight leading, italics for the second half of a headline ("a second AE.", "Let AI do the rest.").
- Body/UI: Instrument Sans 400/500.
- Data: JetBrains Mono for timestamps, field values, prices in the nav, eyebrow labels in tables. Mono signals "this came from the system".
- Headline pattern: plain statement, then the italic turn. "Your closer talks. *CHRM does the rest.*"

## Voice
- Short declaratives. No exclamation marks. No "supercharge", "unlock", "seamless", "revolutionary".
- Name the enemy precisely: the implementation industrial complex, the workflow canvas, the six-week feedback loop.
- Be honest about what CHRM is not: not a CRM, not a notetaker, not prospecting, not SOC 2 yet.
- Numbers over adjectives: 31% → ~100%, under five minutes, under one day. No price on the site; it is shared on the call.
- Illustrative stories are always labelled illustrative until real customers replace them.

## Imagery
- Product: real screenshots in the light "console" frame (CHRM header bar, mono meta, caption). The after-the-call feed is the hero visual and the one image that shows the whole job.
- People: only when telling a role story; always captioned as illustrative until real.
- No stock "AI" imagery, no gradients, no glows, no robots.
